# Audio pack licences

Generated by `tools/audio/build_licensed_pack.py`. Every sample in
`crafti.audp` comes from one of the sources below. The Minecraft
assets themselves are **not** used: they carry no licence, which is why
this pack exists.

## Sources

| source | author | licence | page |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Alexander Ehlers - Free Music Pack.zip` | Alexander Ehlers | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/free-music-pack |
| `chicken_sound_effect.zip` | IMadeIt | CC-BY 3.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/chicken-sound-effect |
| `loops.zip` | obscure music (Gichco) | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/pack-of-loopable-game-music |
| `sfx_100_v2.zip` | rubberduck | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2 |
| `pig_*.mp3` | Vinrax | CC-BY 3.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/pig-sfx-pack |

CC0 needs no credit; the CC-BY ones are credited above as their
licences require.

## Coverage

95 licensed clips, 74 generated placeholders, 149 events the engine
references. Events with no licensed sample fall back to a short
generated tone so the pack always builds; each one is marked
`SYNTHETIC` in `tools/audio/licensed_pack_mapping.tsv`.

The pack is **redistributable**. `crafti.audp` is still gitignored only
because it is a 6 MB build product -- nothing about its contents is
encumbered any more.
